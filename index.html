FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How fast is delivery?

Most orders are delivered in 1–5 minutes after we confirm your payment on Telegram. During busy hours it can take a little longer — we always reply.

Which payment apps work?

Any app that supports KHQR: ABA, ACLEDA, Wing, Bakong, Pi Pay, TrueMoney and most Cambodian banking apps. You scan the code we generate and confirm in your own app.

What information do you need?

Only your Roblox username, the pack you bought, and your payment receipt. Never share your Roblox password or login email — we will never ask for it.

What happens if my QR expires?

KHQR codes expire after 15 minutes for security. Open “My orders”, pick the order and generate a fresh code with the same amount and order ID.

Can I buy Robux for a friend?

Yes. Just enter your friend’s Roblox username at checkout. Delivery goes to that exact username, so please verify it before paying.

What if I send the wrong amount or username?

Message us on Telegram right away with your order ID and receipt. We can fix an unpaid order instantly; a wrong username that was already delivered cannot be reversed.
